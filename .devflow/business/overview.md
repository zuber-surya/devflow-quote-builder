# Business Overview

**Product:** Quote & Invoice Builder — MVP web + mobile app for freelancers and small businesses.

**Core value:** manage the flow Customer → Quote → Invoice → Payment, with professional PDF output, without needing accounting software.

**Primary users:** solo freelancers and small-business owners in India (default currency INR ₹, GST-aware). Single user per account — no team/multi-user accounts in V1.

**Core loop (Core MVP Flow):**
Register → Business Profile → Add Customer → Add Product → Create Quote → Generate PDF → Send/Share → Accept → Convert to Invoice → Generate Invoice PDF → Receive Payment → Mark Paid.

**Explicitly not the product (V1):** not an accounting system, not a payments processor, not a CRM with automation, not multi-currency, not multi-user/team software. See `requirements/functional.md` for the full out-of-scope list.

**Development model:** solo developer using Claude Code, flexible 12+ week timeline, 6 phases (Foundation → Auth → Business Profile → Customers/Products → Quotes → Invoices → PDF/Polish).
